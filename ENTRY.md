# Entry — Dad Joke Showdown

## Winning joke

"I told my son to embrace his mistakes. He gave me a hug."

Character count: 56 (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace).

## Losing variants

1. "I told my son to stop impersonating a flamingo. He had to put his foot down." — a common dad-joke variant; "put his foot down" is heard often.
2. "I have a fear of speed bumps, but I'm slowly getting over it." — solid pun, but wordier setup and less warm around the table.
3. "What do you call a fish wearing a bowtie? Sofishticated." — cute, but the pun word carries the whole joke; feels gimmicky.
4. "I wondered why the baseball was getting bigger. Then it hit me." — a well-known dad-joke chestnut; not fresh.

## Why the winner fits the judge criteria

1. A father would tell it at the table: gentle, family-friendly, about a parent and child.
2. Lands its pun cleanly: "embrace his mistakes" resolving to an actual hug is understood on first read.
3. Feels fresh: not one of the overused dad-joke chestnuts.
4. Tight: every word earns its place and the punchline ("hug") arrives at the end.
5. Reads naturally aloud: speakable in one breath with a rhythm a father could deliver.
